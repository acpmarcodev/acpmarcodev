### Marco Pisani

I build and operate the products of [Torphi Contise](https://github.com/torphidev),
a Brazilian software company in business since 2003.

**[BAYBO](https://baybo.app)** is a personal net worth tracker published on the
App Store and Google Play. It answers how much a person put in, how much they
have today, and whether that did better or worse than CDI, Bitcoin or the
dollar. Values are locked at the exchange rate of the day they are recorded and
history is never recalculated.

**[Bullgate](https://bullgate.dev)** is an open-source, mobile-first platform for
identity, access and billing. It covers registration, login, sessions, account
recovery and the hard cases around them, plus catalogue, subscriptions,
one-time purchases and entitlement delivery. Self-hosting is free.

Mostly .NET and Entity Framework on the backend, MySQL and PostgreSQL for data,
React Native and Expo on mobile, Kubernetes and Argo CD for infrastructure.
